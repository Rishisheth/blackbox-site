The work only you can do
is getting harder to do.

As AI takes on routine work, what's left is the part that needs deep focus and good judgment: deciding what matters, writing what you actually think, solving the hard problem.

Those are exactly the skills an always-on desk wears down. Every ping teaches your attention to break, and judgment only grows from long, honest stretches with hard work and good feedback.

Blackbox builds both on purpose. Daily blocks of real focus build the habit. Reviews grounded in the best thinking on your craft build the judgment. Your record shows it compounding.

Questions

Will it block something I need?
You see the allowed list before you start and can change it with one tap, even mid-block after a 10-second pause.
Do I have to put money on it?
No. Stakes are optional. If you add one, it's lost only if you quit early, wander off again after the warning, or miss a recurring block. You get 24 hours to dispute, and lost stakes go to a charity you choose.
What can Blackbox see?
The app in front, the site's domain and the window title, only during a block. Never your screen or what you type. Reviews are off until you turn them on.
What if I need to stop?
Stop any time. Without a stake, nothing happens. With one, the stake is lost, and you have 24 hours to dispute it if something went wrong.
What if I miss a recurring block?
It counts as missed, and its stake (if you set one) goes to your charity. Pause the schedule ahead of time for holidays and it won't count.
Who is it for?
Anyone whose best work needs long, unbroken focus: engineers, writers, designers, researchers. Mac only for now.
