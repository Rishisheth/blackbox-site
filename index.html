Focus is a muscle.
Blackbox trains it.

Your stamina is the length of deep focus you can reliably hold. Keep three blocks at a length and the coach offers a longer one. Have a rough week and it helps you rebuild, without shame.

  • A plan, not a timer. The coach picks your next block length.
  • Same time, every day. Make any block recurring. It starts on schedule, with no re-planning.
  • Your best hours. One quiet nudge when your focus window opens.
  • Healthy limits. Four hours is a full deep-work day. Rest days never break the streak.

Why it works

Decide once, not all day

You decide once, before you start. Then the Mac holds the line, so you don't spend focus resisting.

A promise with weight

Optionally stake money on your word. Lost stakes go to a charity you choose; we never profit from a miss.

Feedback that compounds

The coach learns your repos, your team's standards and your patterns. Each block teaches the next.

Fair with money. A stake is lost only if you quit early or stray again after the warning. 24 hours to dispute. Lost stakes go to charity.

Private. Your screen and keystrokes never leave your Mac. Reviews are off until you turn them on.
